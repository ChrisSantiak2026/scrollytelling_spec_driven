# 02 // Neural Momentum: Physics-Informed Proxies
## Modeling Rotational Inertia via Neural Simulations.

![split](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1635070041078-e363dbe005cb?q=80&w=2070)

This project leverages my research in **Linear Momentum** and **Rotational Inertia** to build real-time physics simulations. By training a proxy model on experimental lab data, I created a lightweight engine capable of predicting rotational dynamics without the overhead of traditional CAD software.

The core objective is to maintain the integrity of the total kinetic energy equation:
$$K_{total} = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 + \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2$$

* **Work to be Known For**: Precision-focused engineering that respects physical constants while leveraging the speed of neural computation.
* **AI Workflow**: Using AI to find the optimal moment of inertia ($I$) across heterogeneous datasets, automating the calibration process of 3D Blender rigs.
* **Skills**: Statistical analysis, Blender rigging (Mustard UI), and LaTeX-driven technical documentation.
